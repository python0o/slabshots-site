05 — FAQ

Questions

Straight answers from the packages above.

What do I get in the Starter Pack?

Three vertical clips, 15–45 seconds each, ready to post, for $150. That includes one on-site shoot of about 60–90 minutes at a time you choose. Each clip is 9:16 for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts, with burned-in captions, a suggested post caption and hashtags, original royalty-free music, and your logo or shop name and address on the end card. One revision round per clip. Delivered in 5 business days as an MP4 at 1080×1920.

How do payment and delivery work?

$75 to book and $75 on delivery, by Venmo, Zelle, or invoice. Clips arrive within 5 business days of the shoot. You post them, or I can schedule them if you share access. Scheduling is optional.

What do monthly clips and an event recap cost?

Monthly is $275 for 6 clips from one visit. An event recap — a prerelease or tournament — is $120 for 2 clips within 48 hours.

Who owns the clips, and will people be on camera?

You own the finished clips for your shop’s marketing. Customers who appear on camera are asked first, and faces can be avoided on request.

What do websites cost?

Website Refresh starts at $350: restyle the site you have for phones, up to 5 pages, with a working contact form and links to Google Business and Instagram. A new one-page site is $450, with hours, a map, a contact form, social links, and an embedded video. A new site up to 5 pages is $850. The final price depends on what you need. Ask for a free mockup first.

Why is the bundle the best value?

The Video Starter Pack plus a new one-page site is $550. Separately those are $150 and $450, so the bundle saves $50. It’s the best-value way to start with both a video and a site.

What is the launch offer?

$100 off the first 3 sites, in exchange for a testimonial. It applies to websites. Prices listed on this page are before that offer. There are no testimonials on this page — the sample reel is not client work.

What does monthly upkeep include?

Upkeep is optional. $49 a month or $490 a year covers hosting, SSL, monthly checks, and up to 30 minutes of edits. Extra work is $40 an hour.

Where do you work, and how do we start?

I’m a local collector (Pokémon, graded cards) based in Tewksbury, and I work with card shops, game stores, and other small businesses around Tewksbury, Lowell, and Nashua. Video starts with a 15-minute call or visit to pick 3 clip ideas and a shoot time, then one visit with a phone, a small mic, and a light — no crew and no disruption.
